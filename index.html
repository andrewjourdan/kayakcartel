Our mission

Bring every kayak rental company and dealer in America into one crew: one booking system, one referral network, one voice on the water.

Kayak Cartel is franchising America's paddle outfitters. Independent liveries, rental docks and dealers keep their own launches, guides and local knowledge. In return they join a national network where every member recommends every other member, every booking runs through a single system, and every social channel feeds the same story.

A paddler should be able to book any water in any state in one place. We are bringing the major kayak, canoe, paddle board and whitewater rafting companies behind the whole network as sponsors, so the gear, the training and the trips that reach paddlers are backed from the factory to the put-in.

How the network works

Four lines tie the crew together

Every franchise dealer stays a local business. What changes is everything that connects them.

Dealer referrals

Every dealer recommends the rest of the crew. Booked out, wrong water, or a customer heading three states over: the paddler goes to another member, never to a stranger.

One booking system

A single reservation platform for every boat, board, trip and tour in the network. One account, one checkout, live availability at every launch.

Linked social channels

Every dealer's Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube connect to the national Kayak Cartel accounts. A local trip post reaches the whole crew's audience.

National sponsors

The major kayak, canoe, paddle board and rafting companies sponsor the network as a whole, which puts their fleets, gear programs and support behind every member at once.

How a booking moves through the crew

  1. Paddler searchesPicks a state, the water, and what they want to do: fish, tour, run whitewater, or just rent a board.
  2. Nearest dealer answersThe closest member with the right boats and open slots takes the booking.
  3. Crew covers the gapsIf that dealer is full or the trip is outside their water, the system routes it to the next member down the river.
  4. Story goes nationalThe trip photos land on the dealer's channels and the national feed, building the next booking.
